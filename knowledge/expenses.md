# Expense Knowledge

Scope: Germany only at the moment. Your company-specific rules belong in `profile/profile.md`.

## Required information

- Start date and time of outward journey
- Destination (office location with address)
- Arrival date and time
- Start date and time of return journey
- Arrival date and time of return journey
- Business purpose of the trip (a few words are enough)
- Meals paid by the company (if applicable)
